North America Retail Sales Analysis

Where is the retail business performing well, where are delivery and profitability differences appearing, and which areas deserve deeper investigation?

01 · The problem

A retail dataset with three management questions.

The analysis focused on operational performance, profitability and product range. The objective was to identify meaningful differences without pretending that descriptive analysis alone explains their cause.

02 · The approach

Restructuring first, querying second.

The source data included customers, orders, products, locations, returns, profit and fulfilment dates. It was reorganised into fact and dimension tables, relationships were mapped in an ERD, and SQL queries were then used to compare delivery intervals, customer segments, product categories and profitability.

03 · Key findings

The averages were similar. The extremes were not.

Delivery

Average delivery time ranged from 31 days for Home Office customers to 35 days for Corporate customers. But individual records extended from 0 to 214 days—large enough to justify a targeted data-quality and operational investigation.

Profitability

Chairs generated $36,471.10 in positive profit, approximately 4.4 times the $8,358.33 generated by Tables. The Consumer segment produced the highest total profit at $35,427.03.

Product variety

Furnishings had 184 distinct products while Tables had 34. This difference does not itself prove that the smaller range should be expanded.

Strong finding, careful claimA 214-day delivery record is a red flag, not automatic proof of an operational failure. It could reflect fulfilment, returns, order processing or a data-entry issue.

04 · Recommendations

Investigate before optimising.

  • Audit zero-day and 214-day records by product, region, shipping method and order status.
  • Break segment profit into order count, average order value, purchase frequency and profit per order.
  • Investigate the drivers behind chair profitability before reallocating resources.
  • Test whether product-range size is related to sales and margin performance.

05 · Limitations

Descriptive differences are starting points.

The project identifies where performance differs but does not establish why. Conclusions about fulfilment quality, customer preference or assortment strategy require additional operational variables and validation with business stakeholders.
